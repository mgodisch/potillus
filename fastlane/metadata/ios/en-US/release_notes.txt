VoiceOver now carries the whole app. Charts speak one sentence per bar or column, the category ring hands its figures to the legend, and calendar days say their date, their limit status and their grams; an empty day says so. Limit bars, the Today rows and the statistics figures read as sentences with their units written out, the trend as a percentage. Entry rows lead with their time, section titles announce themselves as headings, and the weekday letters and day numbers stay silent so a day is read once. Wording in French, Japanese, Korean and Chinese was corrected in four spoken labels.

The calendar has a year view: a heat-map of every day since your statistics start, with a month and year toggle, where tapping a month opens it. The Today screen shows how long your current alcohol-free streak has run. The statistics screen names the period it shows and moves through it with two arrows, and the start date can be cleared back to your whole history. A backup export can now leave the settings out.

Alcohol-free entries make a dry day: it is no drink day, breaks no streak and stays uncoloured in both calendars. The consumption chart names weekdays, days or months, the time-of-day axis names each bucket’s starting hour, and the longest abstinence reads green while it stands. The PDF report covers the period you pick in the export dialog and lists every month it spans. Limits, drinking days and body weight take a typed value, the time picker offers hours and minutes, and statistics figures sit flush right. The import dialog says what each mode does with your drink list, and gram-limit bars turn red only past the limit. The calendar opens on the current month with no day selected, tapping a drink in edit mode opens the editor, and the entry sheet opens on the drink you logged last.

Fixes: paging a month, a year or a statistics period from its header; the last day of a past period counting towards its average and its chart; an entry’s clock time after a change of time zone or a daylight-saving switch; the blood-alcohol estimate across a gap between rounds; the weekday chart averaging over every occurrence of the weekday; the security footer following the in-app language; and lists scrolling their last row clear of the add button.
